About me

I'm Inès Djili, a naturopath in Neuilly-sur-Seine. I'll walk alongside you as you find your balance, with a particular focus on fatigue.

I came to naturopathy through a deep curiosity about how the human body works, and through my own experience: several everyday complaints that I was able to ease by changing things like my diet, my sleep, how much I moved and how I handled stress.

That led me to study naturopathy in France from 2021 to 2025, on a FENA-accredited programme at ISUPNAT (Institut Supérieur de Naturopathie). I hold a bachelor's degree in naturopathy from FEDE.

A whole-person approach

Naturopathy never looks at a symptom in isolation. Sleep, food, movement, digestion, daily rhythm, emotions: it's all connected. Rather than covering up what your body is showing you, we look for what needs support underneath.

A naturopath is, above all, a health educator. I explain as we go, so that you leave with a clearer understanding of how your body works and of the physiology behind what you're experiencing.

Together, we look at your daily life to find the changes that will genuinely move you towards your goal: realistic, suited to your life, sustainable and truly good for you.

Specialist support for fatigue

Life and my own experience led me to study fatigue in depth. That's why I now offer specialist support in this area, which was also the subject of my final dissertation.

Fatigue is a symptom with dozens of possible causes, and it shows up differently in each of us. That's why it needs a truly individual approach.

Frequently asked questions

What is naturopathy?

Naturopathy is a lifestyle-based approach to well-being. It looks at the whole person and aims to support the body's natural ability to find its balance, through everyday habits such as diet, sleep, movement, stress management and digestion.

Who is it for?

Anyone dealing with ongoing discomfort without finding a satisfying answer, going through a demanding stage of life, or simply wanting to look after themselves before problems arise. You can also come just to understand your body better and fine-tune your habits. In short, naturopathy is for everyone: men and women of all ages.

Does naturopathy replace seeing a doctor?

No. Naturopathy can complement medical care, but never replace it.

Can I have a session online?

No. All sessions take place in person, at the practice.

Is it covered by insurance?

Some French top-up health insurance policies cover part of the cost. Check with your provider; you'll receive an invoice after each session.
